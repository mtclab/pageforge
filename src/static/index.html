What lands in your downloads folder

Most page builders rent you a page on their servers: stop paying, and the page stops existing. pageforge gives you the files instead. This is the whole zip.

  • index.html Your page. Double-click it and it opens, internet or not.
  • style.css The look you picked, as plain CSS you can read and change.
  • assets/ Your photo, your gallery, a favicon drawn from your name.
  • site.json Your answers, beside the website folder - not part of the site. Load it back here any time to keep editing, but do not upload it.
  • README.md How to put it online, with the free hosts written out.
